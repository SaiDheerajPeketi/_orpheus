# Privacy Policy for Orpheus

**Effective date:** September 24, 2026  
**Developer:** Black and Blue

Orpheus is an Android music player for audio stored on your device, published by Black and Blue ("Orpheus," "we," "us," or "our"). This policy explains what the app accesses, what may leave your device, and the choices available to you.

Orpheus does not require an account. We do not sell personal data or show advertising. The app uses Firebase Analytics and Firebase Crashlytics for limited product analytics, reliability monitoring, and crash diagnosis. Advertising ID collection and ad-personalization signals are disabled.

## 1. Information processed on your device

Orpheus processes the following information locally to provide features you choose:

- **Music library:** with your permission, Orpheus reads audio files and media-library metadata such as track title, artist, album, duration, file location, and artwork so it can display and play your music. Audio contents are not uploaded to Black and Blue.
- **Library activity and preferences:** playlists, queue order, favourites, dislikes, ratings, playback position, lyrics timing adjustments, equalizer and playback settings, sleep timers, and app preferences are stored locally.
- **Audio visualization:** if you explicitly enable the beat visualizer, Orpheus asks for Android's audio-capture permission. The app uses Android's Visualizer API only to read frequency data from audio playing in Orpheus and draw the visualization on your device. It does not record, store, or transmit microphone audio.
- **Notifications and playback:** Orpheus uses a foreground media-playback service and notifications to keep user-initiated playback active and provide playback controls when the app is not in the foreground.
- **Audio files opened from other apps:** if you choose Orpheus from Android's Open with interface, the app receives the selected file URI and uses it locally for playback.

This locally processed information is not sent to a Black and Blue server.

## 2. Information sent to service providers

### Optional tips: Google Play Billing and RevenueCat

Orpheus uses Google Play Billing and RevenueCat to offer optional repeatable one-time tips of approximately USD 1, USD 10, and USD 100, with local prices set by Google Play. These services may process purchase history, purchase tokens, transaction status, an anonymous RevenueCat app-user identifier, app and device or operating-system information, and network information such as an IP address.

Tips do not unlock features, content, or access. Payment-card and billing-address handling is performed by Google Play, not Orpheus. Data is handled under the [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy), [Google Play Terms](https://play.google.com/about/play-terms/), and [RevenueCat Privacy Policy](https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy/).

### Firebase Analytics and Crashlytics

Orpheus uses Google Firebase Analytics to understand basic app operation and Firebase Crashlytics to diagnose crashes and non-fatal errors. Analytics may automatically process an app-instance identifier, a masked IP address used to derive approximate location, device and operating-system metadata, app lifecycle events, and Google Play purchase events. Orpheus sets only a fixed technical app-variant property and does not set a Firebase user ID.

Crashlytics may process crash and non-fatal stack traces, relevant app state, app version, device model and operating-system metadata, Crashlytics installation UUIDs, and Firebase installation IDs. Orpheus does not send music files, media-library contents, playlist names, lyrics, microphone audio, or typed text to Firebase through custom analytics or crash fields.

Google processes this information to provide analytics, crash reporting, and service security. Data is encrypted in transit. Orpheus does not link Firebase Analytics to an advertising account, does not use Firebase data for advertising, and removes Android Advertising ID and AdServices identifier permissions from the app. See [Privacy and Security in Firebase](https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy).

### Support and privacy links

When you open the privacy-policy link, your browser and the hosting provider receive ordinary request information such as an IP address and browser metadata. If you email Black and Blue, your email provider and ours process the message and any information you include. Do not include passwords, full purchase tokens, or other sensitive information unless specifically requested through a secure channel.

## 3. Permissions and your choices

Depending on your Android version and the feature you use, Orpheus may request:

- **Music and audio or storage access** to find and play audio stored on your device.
- **Audio capture** only after you choose the beat visualizer and accept an in-app explanation.
- **Notifications** for playback controls and playback status.
- **Foreground media playback and wake lock** to continue user-initiated playback reliably.
- **Internet access** for RevenueCat, Firebase, and opening the privacy page.
- **Google Play Billing** for optional one-time tips.

You can decline or revoke permissions in Android Settings. Music-library access is required for the main local-library experience. Declining audio capture disables only the beat visualizer. Declining notifications may limit playback-control notifications on supported Android versions.

## 4. Retention, security, and deletion

Local data is protected by Android's application sandbox. Orpheus disables Android cloud backup and device-to-device transfer for its private app data. Local data remains until you clear Orpheus storage or uninstall the app; either action deletes the app's private database and preferences. Removing an audio file from your device removes the underlying media independently of Orpheus.

Google Play, RevenueCat, Firebase, email providers, and the privacy-page host retain information according to their own legal, fraud-prevention, security, and operational requirements.

To request help identifying or deleting information associated with an anonymous RevenueCat or Firebase installation identifier, email **developer@blackandblue.co.in** with the subject **Orpheus privacy request**. We may need information from the app or a purchase record to locate the relevant identifier. Some purchase or operational records may need to be retained where required by law, accounting, fraud prevention, security, or dispute handling.

See the dedicated [Orpheus data deletion request page](./delete-data.md) for request steps and the categories of information that may be deleted or retained.

## 5. Children's privacy

Orpheus is intended for people aged 13 and older and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information through a service used by Orpheus, contact us so we can investigate and take appropriate action.
