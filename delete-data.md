# Orpheus data deletion request

Orpheus does not create user accounts. Your music library, playlists, queue, favourites, ratings, lyrics timing, playback settings, and visualizer data stay on your device.

## Delete data stored on your device

You can delete Orpheus's local data at any time by either:

1. Opening Android **Settings > Apps > Orpheus > Storage & cache > Clear storage**, or
2. Uninstalling Orpheus.

Either action deletes the app's private database and preferences. Your original music files remain on your device unless you delete them separately.

## Request deletion of service-linked data

To request help identifying and deleting data associated with an anonymous RevenueCat or Firebase installation identifier:

1. Email **developer@blackandblue.co.in**.
2. Use the subject **Orpheus privacy request**.
3. Describe whether your request concerns a purchase, analytics, or crash-reporting record. If needed, we will reply with instructions for finding the relevant anonymous identifier. Do not send passwords, complete purchase tokens, or payment-card information.

Black and Blue will process a verifiable request and ask its service providers to delete eligible data. Google Play, RevenueCat, Firebase, or Black and Blue may retain limited purchase, security, fraud-prevention, accounting, dispute, or operational records where required by law or legitimate operational requirements.

For more information, read the [Orpheus Privacy Policy](./privacy-policy.md).
